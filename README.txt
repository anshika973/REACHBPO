REACH — Four-page Real Estate BPO Website
========================================

Pages included:
- index.html — Home
- about.html — About REACH
- services.html — Services and FAQ
- contact.html — Contact form

Shared files:
- styles.css — green brand palette and responsive layout
- script.js — mobile navigation, FAQ interactions, and demo form confirmation
- assets/reach-logo.jpeg — supplied REACH logo

Preview:
1. Extract the ZIP to a folder.
2. Open index.html in a browser.
3. Use the shared navigation to move between all four pages.

Branding and functionality notes:
- Main colours use deep emerald green, fresh green, and lime accents to complement the supplied logo.
- The visual direction uses a modern call-centre/customer-support look with team photography and floating support widgets.
- Photos and Google Fonts load from external URLs and require an internet connection.
- The contact form is a front-end demo only. It displays an in-page confirmation but does not transmit or store form data. Connect it to your company email, CRM, or a form backend before going live.
- Company phone, email, and address were not supplied, so they have not been invented.
